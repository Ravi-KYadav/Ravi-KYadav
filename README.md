## 🧠 How I approach an alert

```text
OBSERVE
   ↓
COLLECT EVIDENCE
   ↓
CORRELATE EVENTS + IOCs
   ↓
INVESTIGATE THE STORY
   ↓
ASSESS RISK / IMPACT
   ↓
DOCUMENT CLEARLY
   ↓
ESCALATE • CONTAIN • REMEDIATE
```

I try to keep the same discipline across every project: **start with evidence, avoid assumptions, preserve context, and make the conclusion actionable.**
